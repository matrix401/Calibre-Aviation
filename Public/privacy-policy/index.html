Calibre Aviation (“Calibre Aviation”, “we”, “our”, or “us”) is committed to protecting the privacy and personal information of students, prospective students, parents and guardians, applicants, website visitors, employees, and other individuals who interact with our services. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, retain, and protect personal information when you visit our website, submit an enquiry or application, register for a course, scholarship, talent-search examination, counselling session, screening process, or otherwise communicate with us.

1. SCOPE OF THIS POLICY

This Policy applies to personal information collected through Calibre Aviation’s website, online forms, admission and enquiry processes, telephone calls, email, WhatsApp or other messaging channels, social media interactions, events, seminars, counselling sessions, examinations, and in-person interactions. It applies to information collected directly from you and, where lawfully provided, from parents/guardians, authorised representatives, educational institutions, or service partners.

2. INFORMATION WE MAY COLLECT

Personal and contact information: name, mobile number, email address, residential address, city/state, parent/guardian details, and other contact information.

Educational and application information: date of birth or age, educational qualifications, academic marks, course preferences, career interests, application details, scholarship/examination details, screening information, and documents voluntarily submitted for admission or verification.

Website and technical information: IP address, browser/device type, operating system, pages visited, referring source, approximate usage information, date/time of access, and information collected through cookies or similar technologies.

3. HOW WE COLLECT INFORMATION

We may collect information through website enquiry and registration forms; admission and application forms; scholarship and aviation talent-search registrations; counselling and screening forms; telephone and WhatsApp communications; email; social media; seminars and events; direct visits; and other forms through which you voluntarily provide information.

4. PURPOSES FOR WHICH WE USE INFORMATION

We may use personal information to respond to enquiries; provide aviation education and career counselling; process applications and admissions; administer examinations, scholarships and talent-search programmes; conduct screening and document verification; communicate course, batch, fee, schedule and admission information; provide placement or career assistance where applicable; maintain administrative records; improve our website and services; prevent fraud or misuse; maintain security; comply with legal and regulatory obligations; and send relevant promotional communications where permitted by law and subject to applicable consent or preferences.

5. MARKETING AND COMMUNICATIONS

Where permitted by applicable law, Calibre Aviation may contact you by phone, SMS, WhatsApp, email or other communication channels regarding courses, admissions, scholarships, examinations, events, seminars, aviation career opportunities, or related services. You may request that promotional communications be stopped. Essential administrative communications, such as application, admission, examination, payment or document-verification updates, may still be sent when necessary.

6. COOKIES AND SIMILAR TECHNOLOGIES

Our website may use cookies and similar technologies to remember preferences, maintain functionality, understand website usage, improve performance, measure campaigns, and support security. Where required, we will seek consent for non-essential cookies. You can control cookies through your browser settings, although disabling certain cookies may affect website functionality.

7. SHARING AND DISCLOSURE OF INFORMATION

Calibre Aviation does not sell or rent personal information. Information may be shared, where reasonably necessary and lawful, with authorised employees and representatives; website, hosting, technology and communication providers; payment providers; educational, training, recruitment or placement partners where applicable; professional advisers; auditors; and government, regulatory or law-enforcement authorities where required or permitted by law. We aim to limit disclosures to information reasonably necessary for the relevant purpose.

8. THIRD-PARTY WEBSITES AND SERVICES

Our website may contain links or integrations to third-party websites and services, including payment, social media, analytics, mapping, advertising, communication, or other technology services. Third-party services operate under their own privacy policies and terms. Calibre Aviation is not responsible for privacy practices of third parties that it does not control.

9. DATA SECURITY

We use reasonable technical and organisational safeguards designed to protect personal information against unauthorised access, loss, misuse, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. However, no electronic transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure. You should avoid submitting unnecessary confidential information through public or unsecured channels.

10. DATA RETENTION

We retain personal information for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes for which it was collected, including educational, admission, administrative, accounting, legal, regulatory, dispute-resolution, and legitimate business requirements. When information is no longer required, we may securely delete, anonymise, or otherwise dispose of it in accordance with applicable law and our retention practices.

11. YOUR PRIVACY RIGHTS

Subject to applicable law, you may have rights to request access to certain personal information, request correction of inaccurate information, request deletion where legally permissible, withdraw consent where processing is based on consent, and raise a complaint or concern regarding our handling of personal information. Requests may be subject to identity verification and legal limitations.

12. CHILDREN AND MINORS

Because aviation education and career programmes may involve students below 18 years of age, Calibre Aviation may seek appropriate involvement or consent from a parent or legal guardian where required by applicable law. Parents or legal guardians may contact us regarding a minor’s personal information.

13. PAYMENT INFORMATION

Where payments are processed through a third-party payment gateway, payment information may be processed by that provider. Calibre Aviation does not ordinarily require or intend to store complete card numbers, CVV numbers, passwords, or other sensitive payment credentials on its own systems. Users should review the applicable payment provider’s terms and privacy policy.

14. FRAUD PREVENTION, SECURITY AND LEGAL COMPLIANCE

We may process or disclose information where reasonably necessary to detect or prevent fraud, protect students, staff and systems, investigate misuse, enforce our policies, respond to lawful requests, comply with court orders, or meet applicable legal and regulatory requirements.

15. INTERNATIONAL PROCESSING

Some hosting, technology, analytics, communication, or other service providers may process information outside India. Where applicable, Calibre Aviation will take reasonable steps to ensure that such processing is carried out in accordance with applicable law and appropriate safeguards.

16. CHANGES TO THIS PRIVACY POLICY

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our services, technology, legal requirements, or privacy practices. The latest version will be published on the Calibre Aviation website with the revised “Last Updated” date. We encourage users to review this page periodically.
